Frequently Asked Questions

How do payouts work?

expand_more

Payouts are processed 24 hours after the booking is completed. You can see your balance in the Earnings tab and request a transfer to your linked bank account.

How to list my parking spot?

expand_more

Go to the 'Host' tab, tap on 'Add Listing', and follow the prompts to upload photos, set your price, and define availability.

What if a driver stays too long?

expand_more

You can report an overstay through the active booking screen. Our system will automatically apply additional charges based on the extra time.

How do I cancel my booking?

expand_more

Open your 'Bookings' tab, select the reservation, and tap 'Cancel'. Refunds are subject to the cancellation policy set by the host.
